# Feature Signals

- Tempo helps signal pacing. Faster tempo usually fits intense, workout, or dance-oriented requests; slower tempo usually fits calm, ambient, or reflective requests.
- Valence helps signal emotional color. Higher valence usually fits happy, uplifting, or euphoric requests; lower valence usually fits moody, melancholic, or aggressive requests.
- Danceability helps signal movement and groove. Higher danceability often fits pop, funk, latin, electronic, and workout requests.
- Energy helps signal intensity. Higher energy often fits rock, electronic, metal, or gym contexts; lower energy often fits lofi, ambient, classical, or study contexts.
- No single feature is enough on its own. Good matches usually align across mood, target energy, and at least one supporting signal like tempo, danceability, valence, or acousticness.
