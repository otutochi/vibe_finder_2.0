# Mood Traits

- Happy: often pairs with higher valence, brighter tone, and moderate-to-high danceability.
- Chill: often pairs with lower energy, smoother pacing, and softer dynamics.
- Focused: often pairs with steady rhythm, low distraction, and moderate or low energy.
- Intense: often pairs with fast tempo, strong momentum, and higher energy.
- Relaxed: often pairs with gentle pacing, warm tone, and lower energy.
- Moody: often pairs with darker tone, moderate energy, and lower valence.
- Nostalgic: often pairs with warm tone and moderate valence rather than extreme highs or lows.
- Euphoric: often pairs with high energy, high valence, and strong rhythmic drive.
- Melancholic: often pairs with lower valence and reflective tone.
- Dreamy: often pairs with slow tempo, soft texture, and low-to-mid energy.
- Aggressive: often pairs with very high energy, sharper attack, and lower valence.
- Groovy: often pairs with high danceability and a clear rhythmic pocket.
- Uplifting: often pairs with positive valence and moderate-to-high energy.
- Romantic: often pairs with warm tone, smoother pacing, and moderate valence.
