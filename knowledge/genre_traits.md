# Genre Traits

- Pop: usually bright, catchy, and moderately high energy. Often pairs well with happy moods, strong hooks, and above-average danceability.
- Lofi: usually low-to-mid energy, soft, and steady. Often fits studying, focusing, or quiet background listening.
- Rock: usually higher energy with stronger drums and guitars. Often fits intense or driving moods with faster tempo.
- Ambient: usually slow, spacious, and very low energy. Often fits chill, dreamy, or calm listening.
- Jazz: usually relaxed and warm with moderate tempo variation. Often fits reflective or relaxed listening.
- Electronic: usually high energy, synthetic, and rhythm-forward. Often fits workouts, dance-heavy listening, or euphoric moods.
- Hip hop: often groove-led with strong rhythm and lyrical focus. Energy can range from reflective to confident, but danceability is often moderate to high.
- Classical: often low energy and acoustic with low danceability. Often fits reading, relaxing, or reflective listening.
- Metal: usually very high energy, aggressive, and fast. Often fits intense or aggressive moods.
- Funk and latin: often groove-heavy, upbeat, and highly danceable. Often fit social, uplifting, or movement-focused listening.
