# Listening Contexts

- Studying: usually benefits from focused or chill moods, lower energy, steadier tempo, and often higher acousticness.
- Working out: usually benefits from high energy, faster tempo, and stronger rhythmic drive.
- Relaxing: usually benefits from chill, relaxed, or dreamy moods with lower tempo and softer dynamics.
- Focusing: usually benefits from moderate or low energy, stable rhythm, and low-distraction mood cues.
- Commuting: often works well with upbeat, confident, or moderately energetic tracks that keep momentum without being chaotic.
- Late night listening: often fits moody, chill, nostalgic, or dreamy songs with moderate or lower energy.
