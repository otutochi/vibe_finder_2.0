# Acousticness

- High acousticness usually suggests softer instrumentation, less synthetic production, and a more unplugged feel.
- Acoustic songs often work well for studying, reading, coffee-shop listening, and calm background listening.
- Low acousticness usually suggests heavier electronic production or amplified instrumentation.
- Low acousticness can still fit calm moods, but it more often supports gym, commute, nightlife, or high-energy listening.
- Acoustic preference should be treated as a fit signal, not the only deciding factor; mood and target energy still matter.
